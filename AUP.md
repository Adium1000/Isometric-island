# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

By using this game, you agree to follow these rules:

### 1. Allowed Use

- Create and share creative islands
- Use the game for fun and relaxation
- Share your creations with others

### 2. Prohibited Use
You are NOT allowed to:

- Create or share explicit or inappropriate content
- Upload empty, spam, or low-effort maps
- Copy or steal other people’s work without credit
- Exploit bugs or break the game intentionally
- Harass or abuse other users

### 3. Enforcement

- Content that breaks these rules may be removed
- Users who violate rules may lose access to community features

### 4. Notes

- This game is made for creative and relaxing purposes. Respect the community and have fun!
