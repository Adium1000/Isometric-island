# Privacy Policy
Last Updated: April 2026

### 1. Introduction
- Isometric Island is an isometric building game that runs entirely within your web browser. This Privacy Policy explains how data is managed within the game.
While the core game data is kept on your device, optional cloud features (such as account creation and island publishing) involve the processing of your email address via Google Firebase.

### 2.Stored Data 

Local Storage
The game utilizes your browser's localStorage mechanism to save the following types of information:

- Saved Islands
- Visual Settings 
- Custom Key Bindings
- Achievements
- Audio Preferences

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us or any third parties. You can delete it at any time through your browser settings

Cloud Storage
- In addition to local storage, Isometric Island offers optional cloud saving features powered by Google Firebase.
- When you choose to publish an island, the layout data is stored in our cloud database and becomes accessible to other users via the sharing feature.
Data Collected: If you create an account, we collect and store your email address and a unique User ID.
Purpose: This data is used solely to authenticate your identity, allow you to publish your islands.
Legal Basis: Processing is based on your consent and is necessary to provide the account synchronization service you requested.
Cloud (Firebase): Account data (email) and islands saved in the cloud are stored for as long as the account is active. If you request account deletion, your personal data is immediately removed from our active database, but may remain in secure backups for a period of 30 days.

### 3. Island Sharing Feature
 The game offers an optional feature to share islands via a URL. When you generate a sharing link, your island data is encoded directly into that URL.
- Important: Anyone who receives the sharing link can view and load your island. Do not send links containing private islands to individuals you do not trust. Isometric Island does not store these links on its own servers.

### 4. Third-Party Services
The game loads resources from the following external providers. Each provider applies its own privacy policy, independent of Isometric Island:

    - Google Fonts: The game uses the "Press Start 2P" font, loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). By loading the page, your browser makes an HTTP request to Google's servers, which may include your device's IP address.
    - Spotify: The game integrates an embedded Spotify player in the music section. If you interact with this player, Spotify may place cookies and collect data according to its own policy.
    - GitHub: The game makes an HTTP request to raw.githubusercontent.com to load the project's README file. GitHub may log this request according to its own policy.
    - Hosting Provider: Our game is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosting services, they may automatically collect server logs, which include technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and time of access. These logs are managed by the provider according to their own privacy policies.
    - Google Firebase: Acts as our data processor for authentication and database management. Your data is stored on secure servers and is protected according to Google’s privacy standards and industry-best encryption practices. Please note that Google Firebase may store and process data in various locations globally. By using the cloud features, you consent to the transfer of your information to facilities located outside of your country of residence.

### 5. Cookies
- Although we do not use our own cookies for tracking purposes, we use LocalStorage to ensure the game’s technical functionality (saves). These are strictly necessary and do not require consent under current regulations
- The third-party services mentioned in Section 4 (Google, Spotify, GitHub) may use their own cookies in accordance with their respective privacy policies. We recommend consulting them for detailed information.

### 6. Your Rights (Right to Erasure) (GDPR)
- In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights regarding your data:
- Right to Erasure (Cloud Data): You can permanently delete your account and all associated email data directly through the "Delete Account" option in the game settings.
- Right to Erasure (Local Data): You can delete all local data via DevTools (F12) > Application > Local Storage > Clear All or through your browser settings.
- Right of Access & Rectification: You can view and modify your settings and saved islands directly within the game interface.
- Legal basis for local storage: The legitimate interest of the user to save their game progress (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### 7. Minimum Age
Isometric Island is not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not intentionally collect data from minors.
Discovery of Data: If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a user under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete that information and the associated Firebase account from our servers.

### 9. Data Security
We take the security of your data seriously. We use Firebase Security Rules to ensure that only you can access your personal account data and saved islands. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.


### 10. Account Termination and Content Removal
We reserve the right, at our sole discretion, to terminate or suspend access to our cloud services (Firebase accounts) and remove any published content (islands) without prior notice or liability. This may occur in cases of:

- Abuse: Technical manipulation of the game or cloud services.
- Inappropriate Content: Publishing islands that contain offensive, illegal, or harmful visual structures.
- System Integrity: Actions that threaten the stability of the Firebase database or the game's infrastructure.
